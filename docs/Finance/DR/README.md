## Instrument Space

### Government Financial Instrument Space

| **Financial Instrument**          | **Description**                                                                          | **Instrument Tree Path**                                                   | **Emisor**                                      | **Usage**                      |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **Bono del Tesoro**               | Debt security issued to obtain government financing.                                     | Government Instrument → Debt → Government Bond → Treasury Bond             | **Ministerio de Hacienda / Gobierno Central**   | Government financing           |
| **Letra del Tesoro**              | Short-term government debt security.                                                     | Government Instrument → Debt → Government Bill → Treasury Bill             | **Ministerio de Hacienda / Gobierno Central**   | Short-term financing           |
| **Bono Soberano**                 | Debt security representing an obligation of the Dominican sovereign.                     | Government Instrument → Debt → Sovereign Bond                              | **Gobierno de la República Dominicana**         | Sovereign financing            |
| **Bono Global**                   | Sovereign debt security issued in international capital markets.                         | Government Instrument → Debt → Sovereign Bond → Global Bond                | **Gobierno de la República Dominicana**         | International financing        |
| **Bono Internacional**            | Government debt security issued in an international capital market.                      | Government Instrument → Debt → Sovereign Bond → International Bond         | **Gobierno de la República Dominicana**         | International financing        |
| **Préstamo Soberano**             | Loan contracted by the government with a creditor.                                       | Government Instrument → Debt → Government Loan → Sovereign Loan            | **Gobierno de la República Dominicana**         | Government financing           |
| **Crédito Multilateral**          | Financing provided by a multilateral development institution to the government.          | Government Instrument → Debt → Government Loan → Multilateral Credit       | **Gobierno de la República Dominicana**         | Development financing          |
| **Crédito Bilateral**             | Financing provided by another state or bilateral institution.                            | Government Instrument → Debt → Government Loan → Bilateral Credit          | **Gobierno de la República Dominicana**         | Government financing           |
| **Crédito Sindicado**             | Credit facility provided collectively by multiple lenders.                               | Government Instrument → Debt → Syndicated Credit                           | **Gobierno / entidad pública**                  | Large-scale financing          |
| **Certificado del Banco Central** | Debt security issued by the central bank.                                                | Government Instrument → Central Bank Instrument → Central Bank Certificate | **Banco Central de la República Dominicana**    | Monetary/liquidity management  |
| **Nota del Banco Central**        | Debt security issued by the central bank.                                                | Government Instrument → Central Bank Instrument → Central Bank Note        | **Banco Central de la República Dominicana**    | Monetary/liquidity management  |
| **Reporto**                       | Transaction involving securities and an agreement to reverse the transaction.            | Government Instrument → Financing → Repo                                   | **Entidad participante**                        | Short-term financing/liquidity |
| **Garantía Soberana**             | Government guarantee supporting an obligation of another entity.                         | Government Instrument → Credit → Sovereign Guarantee                       | **Gobierno de la República Dominicana**         | Credit enhancement             |
| **Aval del Estado**               | State-backed guarantee of a financial obligation.                                        | Government Instrument → Credit → State Guarantee                           | **Gobierno de la República Dominicana**         | Credit support                 |
| **Acción**                        | Equity security representing ownership in a company.                                     | Government Instrument → Equity → Share                                     | **Empresa / entidad participada por el Estado** | State ownership                |
| **Participación en Fondo**        | Ownership interest held by a public entity in an investment fund.                        | Government Instrument → Equity → Fund Interest                             | **Fondo / vehículo correspondiente**            | Public investment              |
| **Valor de Fideicomiso**          | Financial claim or security associated with a trust structure.                           | Government Instrument → Structured → Trust Security                        | **Fideicomiso / emisor correspondiente**        | Structured financing           |
| **Certificado de Participación**  | Security representing a participation or beneficial interest in an underlying structure. | Government Instrument → Structured → Participation Certificate             | **Emisor / fideicomiso correspondiente**        | Structured investment          |
